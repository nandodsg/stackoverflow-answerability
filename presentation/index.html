    { 05 · prompt 1 }

    Which tags get answers?

    In 2022, text processing and data wrangling win: jq, awk, sed, regex, dplyr, data.table. Between 58% and 68% of their questions end with an accepted answer (the whole year: 30.5%), and awk collects 2.45 answers per question. Platforms and integrations lose: magento2, appium, facebook, Chrome extensions, about one question in ten accepted. Pairs tell the same story, except the #1, arrays + google-sheets, which exists mostly because of retagging after the answer.

    Ranked by the 95% interval, with a 385-question floor (1,162 tags, 1,026 pairs). Full lists and caveats: README, section 1. The panel runs on placeholder files until the marts are exported. Edit the query: try n_questions >= 100 or creation_year = 2021, then Run.

    { HOW WE KNOW }

    EvidenceEDA
    Unfiltered, the 2022 top and bottom 10 are tags with 1–2 questions at 100% and 0%. 385 is the smallest n with a ±5-point worst-case margin. Raw-rate and empirical-Bayes rankings share 9–10 of 10 names with these lists, and 2021 keeps 8 of the top 10 tags. eda/d1_sample_size, eda/d1_answers_ranking.
    Guardtests
    Floor, rate bounds and sort order are dbt tests (Wilson checked against hand-computed values). A warn test reruns the ranking on the original tags: 46 tags and 65 pairs flagged, 9 of 10 tags stay in each list. No row in a top or bottom 10 fails Cochran's rule, so no interval is labeled approximate.
    CostGB billed
    queries/q1.sql bills about 9 GB (8.92 GiB in a fresh sandbox), metadata columns only, never body. The original-tags check reads the one-time post_history text scan (106 GiB, run once).

    { 06 · prompt 2 }

    Python-only vs dbt-only, year over year

    python-only answers per question fell from 2.31 (2013) to 1.52 (2021), and the accepted rate from 65.2% to 43.0%. The 1.26 of 2022 is mostly censoring: questions not answered yet when the data stops. dbt-only has 31, 58 and 79 questions (2020–2022) at 1.39, 1.14 and 1.06, intervals overlapping, so no dbt trend; but in 2021 and 2022 dbt-only got fewer answers per question than python-only. The ratio shown is answers per question; Q÷A = 1 / this (python 2022: 0.79 questions per answer).

    { HOW WE KNOW }

    EvidenceEDA
    2021 → 2022, python-only: answer rate 84.8% → 73.5%, answers per answered question 1.80 → 1.71. About three quarters of the drop is questions not answered yet (censoring). eda/d3_ratio, eda/d2_denominator.
    Guardtests
    A unit test pins the exact-match filter (python, python|pandas, python-3.x, dbt, dbt|sql); empty dbt years stay as rows. A warn test on the original tags flags 3 rows: python 2019 (a near-zero YoY change switches sign), python 2021 (1.52 vs 1.50), dbt 2022 (accepted YoY +2.0 vs −0.6 points).
    CostGB billed
    queries/q2.sql bills about 7 GB, metadata columns only, never body.
